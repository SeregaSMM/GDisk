DESIGN.md Спецификация Moxie Care для ИИ-агентов 
# DESIGN.md — Moxie Care Aesthetic System

## 1. Brand Philosophy & Vibe
- **Aesthetic**: Warm Clinical Minimalism, High-Trust MedSpa, Editorial Healthcare.
- **Tone**: Calm, authoritative, transparent, tactile. Never sterile-cold blue.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Background Primary**: `#FAF7F2` (Warm Alabaster / Soft Cream)
- **Surface / Card**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White with `#E6E1D6` 1px border)
- **Secondary Surface**: `#F2EFE9` (Soft Botanical Sage / Stone)
- **Primary Brand Accent**: `#234233` (Deep Botanical Forest Green)
- **Highlight / Badge Accent**: `#D96B43` (Warm Terracotta)
- **Text Primary**: `#1C2420` (Deep Charcoal Ink)
- **Text Muted**: `#5A655E` (Muted Herb Gray)

## 3. Typography
- **Headings (Serif)**: `Cormorant Garamond` or `Instrument Serif` — weight 600, tight tracking (`-0.01em`), italic accents for key clinical terms.
- **Body & UI (Sans)**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` — weights 400, 500, 600. Clean geometric legibility for long-form medical guides.
- **Metadata / Clinical Passports (Mono)**: `JetBrains Mono` — uppercase tracking for dosage, downtime, and duration metrics.

## 4. Component Rules
- **Cards**: `border-radius: 24px` (`rounded-3xl`), `1px solid #E6E1D6`, subtle warm shadow.
- **Buttons**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), primary in `#234233` with white text.
- **Doctor's Nuance Callout**: Highlighted box in `#F2EFE9` with left border `#234233`.
